# Artificial wormhole experiments (#56)

The selected design is an expensive experiment with an initially **unknown
exit**. Links between two chosen points remain the role of
[stargates](stargate-specialist.md). Experiments use ordinary paid construction
and the existing [transient wormhole](wormholes.md) travel and knowledge rules.

## Prototype generator

| Requirement | Initial balance |
| --- | --- |
| Technology | Energy 26, Construction 24, Electronics 26 |
| Generator | 8000 work, at most 80/year; minimum 100 years |
| Minerals | I 800 / B 400 / G 800 kt, paid as work advances |
| Support | Populated colony and friendly operational Starbase/Fortress shipyard |
| Open rift | 40-year transient pair; one experiment per completed generator |

These are initial balance numbers. The generator occupies the existing one
engineering slot. Normal queue priority, allocation and physical mineral
deliveries apply. All races with the technology can build it, including Native
Orbital Civilization under its usual population and production rules.

Completing the generator leaves it ready. **Activate unknown-exit experiment…**
requires a separate confirmed order; ordinary construction cannot open a rift.
Execution rechecks technology, population and support. Lost support retains the
activation request until support returns. A spent generator cannot activate
again; dismantling and rebuilding pays the entire new recipe.

## Opening and traversal

The host creates one mouth at the generator's system and a seeded, randomly
located distant exit within the map. Generation does not consult private enemy
assets or grant knowledge of the exit. An exit in existing sensor coverage can
be learned through an ordinary observation; otherwise exploration is required.

The result is an ordinary transient pair, with independently moving mouths and
possible relocation, a 40-year lifetime and initial stability 0.65–0.85. It uses
the campaign's usual whole-fleet transit lottery and detector mitigation. Under
default risk rules, unmitigated entry risk initially lies between 2.8% and 5.2%.
Exact stability and a hidden transit outcome are not exposed by planning.

Use the ordinary anomaly journal, flight approach and explicit **Enter wormhole**
arrival action. Proximity never starts transit. Stale coordinates can miss; local
sensors may reacquire a moving mouth. Exits, entry/emergence and collapse use the
existing delayed reports and missing-fleet assessment. Other empires can observe
and enter the same physical anomaly under normal rules.

Opening consumes the generator's experiment. The rift subsequently exists
independently: dismantling the spent machinery, losing the supporting station
or capturing the planet does not close it or reset its age. Constructing another
generator supplies no new information about a previous exit.

## Information and persistence

The engineering panel shows received preparation, activation and opening state,
and the entrance's stable identity when reported. It provides no chosen exit or
future route simulation. The anomaly journal supplies dated contacts and learned
links. Player exports omit physical pairs and undelivered exit knowledge.

Save 132/orders 58 add generator/action state while retaining the existing natural
wormhole record shape, transit risk and knowledge formats. Initial experiments
are controlled explicitly through ordinary player orders. The related
[radical transformation](radical-transformation.md) is the other active #56
direction; remaining ocean/ice and system projects are frozen.
