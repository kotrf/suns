# Radical planetary transformation (#56)

This is an additional late-game project alongside ordinary
[terraforming](terraforming.md). The user-approved sequence is reversible
preparation, explicit irreversible commitment, a long supported transition and
a permanent environment tailored to the initiating race. Residents can remain
in shelters without producing resources, or travel to another world to work.
Support loss pauses the transition without undoing changes.

## Prototype construction

| Requirement | Initial balance |
| --- | --- |
| Race | Uses surface colonies; not Native Orbital Civilization |
| Technology | Energy 26, Construction 26, Biology 24 |
| Preparation | 9600 production work, at most 80/year; minimum 120 years |
| Preparation minerals | I 960 / B 480 / G 960 kt, paid as work advances |
| Transition | 30 supported years after explicit commitment |
| Preparation support | Friendly operational Starbase/Fortress with a shipyard |

These numbers are prototype balance. Preparation uses the normal colony queue,
research allocation and delivered materials. Queue priority, missing supplies or
lost support extend the schedule. It occupies the existing single engineering
slot. Pause/removing the queue item preserves investment; dismantling preparation
loses the investment. Preparation alone changes no environmental axes.

The construction price includes the transition equipment and protected housing.
Finishing preparation enables **Commit irreversible transformation…**; it does
not start the transition automatically. The confirmation explains the loss of
surface output, the fixed target and the inability to cancel or retarget.

## Commitment, shelters and the transition

Commitment records the current initiating race's temperature/gravity/radiation
midpoints, the preceding physical and natural axes, the initiating empire and
the date. Targets remain fixed through capture. Before commitment, a new owner
may finish preparation and initiate the project for its own race.

Every resident present at commitment receives a prepaid shelter place. During
transition, including a pause:

- Surface residents generate no production, ordinary mining, xenocrystals,
  surface research or population growth. The surface is unavailable for ordinary
  terraforming. Existing stocks, buildings and mineral geology are retained.
- Shelters protect residents from transitional environmental attrition. Ordinary
  warfare still affects people; shelters grant no new military immunity.
- Ordinary Cargo and route loading can evacuate every sheltered resident,
  including the last one. They become productive under the destination world's
  usual rules. There is no automatic evacuation or free movement.
- Vacated paid places accept returning residents. Capacity does not expand with
  immigration; excess unloading stays aboard, and an oversized exact transfer
  fails atomically. Invading survivors beyond available shelter capacity remain
  aboard their transport rather than disappearing.

The first transition year resolves in the year of commitment. Each active,
supported year moves the physical axes along the saved original-to-target path.
Repeated processing cannot add another annual step. Pause, loss of the supporting
station or the current owner's operating technology retains the accumulated
changes and remaining work. Dismantling and retargeting are unavailable during
transition, including after capture.

## Restoring support and completion

An inactive surface cannot manufacture a replacement station. A physically
delivered **independent orbital shipyard base** can therefore support an already
committed transition. It must belong to the colony owner, finish its normal
six-year paid assembly, be active and supplied, and survive damage. Its residents,
construction and yearly service costs follow the existing orbital-base rules.
This recovery path supports the prepaid transition; it grants no surface output.

After 30 supported years, the target becomes the planet's permanent environment
and new stable natural baseline. The ordinary economy resumes in the next annual
production cycle. Removing the equipment or losing its station cannot undo the
world. Ordinary terraforming then uses its existing limits around the new
baseline. The original project dates and milestones remain campaign history.

## Controls and received evidence

Planetary engineering offers separate preparation, commitment, pause/resume and
dismantling commands. The panel shows paid work, transition years, the fixed
target and the owner's paid housing capacity. System Details identifies the
closed surface rather than promising ordinary terraforming output.

Physical, owner-received, foreign and in-flight evidence remain separate.
Foreign orbital reports expose the physical project and target but redact the
private housing capacity and investments. Hidden changes cannot alter the
owner's UI or exported turn before delivery. Previews apply current orders and
scalar rules without future-year simulation or polling. Initial control is
explicit through normal player orders; the existing computer planner retains
its current engineering selection policy.

Save 132/orders 58 append the project/action identities and kind-specific state;
previous engineering record layouts remain intact. See also the separate
[artificial wormhole experiment](artificial-wormholes.md). Other unfinished
ocean/ice and system-engineering directions in #56 are frozen.
